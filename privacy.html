Privacy Policy

MonoPump - Heart Rate Monitor

Introduction

Welcome to MonoPump. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy will inform you about how we look after your personal data when you use our application and tell you about your privacy rights and how the law protects you.

Key Principle: MonoPump is designed with privacy as a top priority. All health data is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to our servers without your explicit consent.

Information We Collect

1. Health Data

MonoPump collects and processes the following health data:

  • Heart rate measurements
  • Measurement timestamps
  • User profile information (name, age, gender if provided)

All health data is stored locally on your device only. We do not collect, store, or transmit this data to any external servers.

2. Camera Usage

The app uses your device's camera solely for the purpose of measuring heart rate. Camera access is requested only when you initiate a measurement, and no video or images are stored or transmitted.

3. HealthKit Integration

If you choose to enable HealthKit integration:

  • Heart rate data may be synced with Apple Health on your device
  • This integration is controlled entirely through iOS system settings
  • You can disable HealthKit access at any time in your device settings

How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely for the purpose of providing the app's functionality:

  • To measure and display your heart rate
  • To store and display your measurement history
  • To generate health insights based on your data
  • To export your data when you request it
  • To support multiple user profiles

Data Storage and Security

Local Storage

All data is stored locally on your device using standard iOS data storage mechanisms. Your data is protected by your device's built-in security features.

No Cloud Storage

We do not operate any cloud servers or store your data on external servers. Your data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to export it.

Data Export

When you export data (PDF, Excel, HTML files):

  • Files are generated locally on your device
  • You control how and where to share these files
  • We do not have access to exported files

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not:

  • Sell your personal data
  • Share your personal data with third parties
  • Use your data for advertising purposes
  • Transfer your data to any external servers

The only data sharing occurs when you explicitly export and share files yourself through iOS sharing mechanisms.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access all your data stored in the app
  • Export your data in multiple formats
  • Delete individual measurements or all your data
  • Delete user profiles
  • Disable HealthKit integration at any time

All these actions can be performed directly within the app.

Children's Privacy

MonoPump does not knowingly collect any personal information from children. The app can be used by all ages, but we recommend that minors use the app under adult supervision.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our privacy policy from time to time. When we do, we will notify you through an update to the app or an in-app notification. Your continued use of the app after such changes constitutes your acceptance of the new privacy policy.

We will respond to all legitimate requests within 30 days.
